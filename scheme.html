Professional Development

My professional development has taken place across several distinct but connected environments, including criminal justice, finance, compliance and technology. Although these fields use different tools and terminology, each requires sound judgement, careful handling of information, clear communication and an understanding of how decisions affect people, organisations and wider systems.

My criminal justice experience developed my ability to work within structured procedures while responding to complex and changing circumstances. It involved coordinating with different agencies, assessing information, managing competing priorities and recognising that risks cannot always be understood in isolation from a person's wider situation.

I later applied many of the same capabilities within bookkeeping, payroll and compliance environments, where accuracy, evidence and regulatory deadlines are essential. This work also led me to design practical spreadsheets, checks and software tools to make complicated processes more reliable and visible.

My subsequent development across software engineering, testing, DevOps and cybersecurity has added a technical dimension to this experience. I have completed practical work involving application security, vulnerability assessment, CI/CD controls, network security, database protection and security-event detection.

I am now bringing these areas together through governance, risk and compliance. My particular interest is in understanding how people, processes and technology interact. How weaknesses at the boundaries between them can develop into operational, financial and/or security risks.
